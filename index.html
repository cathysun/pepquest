PepQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

PepQuest is a fitness pet game published by Pixel Stride Games. This policy explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

Step and motion data

PepQuest reads your device's step counter to award in-game Pep. Step counts are processed and stored only on your device. We do not upload, collect, or share your step, motion, health, or fitness data. The app does not connect to Apple Health or Google Fit.

Game progress

Your pet's level, abilities, and battle progress are saved locally on your device. There are no accounts and no server-side storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

PepQuest is free and supported by ads served through third-party advertising SDKs (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads, and AppLovin MAX where enabled). These SDKs may collect device information such as advertising identifiers (IDFA / Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Their handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any advertising identifier is accessed. You can decline and still play the full game; ads will simply be less personalized. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings.

Children

PepQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
